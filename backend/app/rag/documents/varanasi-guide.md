# Varanasi Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Dashashwamedh Ghat & Evening Ganga Aarti** — Sacred primary riverfront ghat where priests perform magnificent choreographed brass-lamp rituals every dusk.
- **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** — Revered golden-spired Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, standing along the sacred western banks of the holy Ganges.
- **Assi Ghat** — Serene southern confluence ghat renowned for sunrise Subah-e-Banaras classical music, yoga, and contemplative boat rides.
- **Manikarnika Ghat** — Ancient sacred cremation ghat embodying centuries-old philosophical traditions of liberation and spiritual eternity.
- **Sarnath Buddhist Sanctuary** — Sacred Deer Park where Lord Buddha delivered his first sermon after enlightenment, featuring Dhamek Stupa and Lion Capital museum.
- **Old Kashi Alleys & Heritage Galis** — Atmospheric maze of medieval stone alleys lined with silk weavers, sacred shrines, and traditional sweet makers.
- **Dawn Ghat Photography & Sunburst Cruise** — Panoramic golden-hour sunrise boat viewpoint capturing iconic morning light and riverfront photography angles.

## Spirituality, Culture & Culinary
- Dawn wooden rowing boat rides along the riverfront catching golden morning reflections across the ghats.
- Famous Banarasi street food: piping hot Kachori Sabzi, creamy Malaiyo saffron milk foam, and Banarasi Paan.
