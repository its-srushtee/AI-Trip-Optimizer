# Tokyo Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Tsukiji Outer Market & Toyosu** — World-famous culinary epicenter teeming with artisan sushi bars, fresh oyster stands, wagyu beef skewers, and tamagoyaki omelet makers.
- **Senso-ji Temple & Asakusa** — Tokyo's oldest and most sacred Buddhist temple (founded in 645 AD), entered through the monumental Kaminarimon thunder gate and Nakamise shopping street.
- **Shibuya Crossing & Shibuya Sky** — The world's most photogenic pedestrian scramble, paired with an open-air 360-degree rooftop observation deck 229m above Shibuya.
- **Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden** — Expansive 144-acre botanical sanctuary blending traditional Japanese landscape, English formal, and French formal garden aesthetics.
- **teamLab Planets & Borderless** — Mind-bending interactive digital art museum where visitors wade through water and walk under crystalline light canopies.
- **Meiji Jingu Shrine & Harajuku** — Peaceful imperial Shinto shrine nestled inside a 170-acre evergreen forest of 120,000 trees, adjacent to trendy Takeshita Street.
- **Omoide Yokocho & Golden Gai** — Atmospheric post-war lantern-lit alleyways packed with intimate 6-seat yakitori taverns, craft cocktail dens, and nostalgic Showa-era charm.
- **Akihabara Electric Town** — Vibrant cultural center for Japanese electronics, anime, gaming arcades, and neon-lit facades.

## Food & Gastronomy
- **Edomae Sushi** — Pristine seasonal nigiri sushi seasoned with red vinegar, crafted right before your eyes by master itamae.
- **Ramen Diversity** — Rich tonkotsu (pork bone) broth, delicate shoyu (soy sauce), and dipping tsukemen at legendary noodle shops across Shinjuku and Ikebukuro.
- **Izakaya Dining** — Charcoal-grilled chicken yakitori skewers, crispy karaage, gyoza, and draft beer in bustling neighborhood taverns.
- **Monjayaki** — Savory pan-fried runny batter packed with cabbage, seafood, and mochi on Tsukishima's dedicated Monja Street.
- **Matcha & Japanese Sweets** — Authentic whisked Uji matcha tea ceremonies and seasonal Wagashi sweets in Asakusa.

## Photography & Viewpoints
- Shibuya Sky rooftop during the transition from golden hour into Tokyo's glowing evening skyline.
- Senso-ji pagoda and red lantern illumination after twilight.
- The kaleidoscopic mirror and infinity LED installations inside teamLab Planets.
- Vibrant neon reflections on rainy nights through Shinjuku's Kabukicho and Omoide Yokocho.

## Travel Tips
- Best season: March to May (cherry blossoms) and September to November (crisp weather and brilliant fall foliage).
- Moderate pace itineraries allow 2 to 3 main neighborhoods per day connected by Tokyo's world-class subway network.
- Pick up a digital Suica or Pasmo IC transport card for seamless tap-and-go travel on trains, buses, and convenience stores.
