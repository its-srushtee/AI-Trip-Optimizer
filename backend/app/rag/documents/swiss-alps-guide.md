# Swiss Alps Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Matterhorn & Zermatt** — Iconic pyramid-shaped alpine colossus rising to 4,478m; car-free alpine village with Gornergrat cogwheel railway and glacier paradise cable cars.
- **Jungfraujoch (Top of Europe)** — Highest railway station in Europe at 3,454m, featuring the Sphinx observation deck, Aletsch Glacier ice palace, and panoramic mountain vistas.
- **Lauterbrunnen Valley & Trümmelbach Falls** — The valley of 72 cascading waterfalls, sheer vertical limestone cliffs, and alpine pastures inspiring Tolkien's Rivendell.
- **Glacier 3000 & Peak Walk** — The world's only suspension bridge connecting two mountain summits, with panoramic views of Mont Blanc, the Matterhorn, and the Bernese Alps.
- **Lake Geneva & Chillon Castle** — Historic medieval island fortress on the shores of Lake Geneva surrounded by terraced Lavaux vineyards and snowcapped peaks.

## Nature & Outdoor Adventure
- Scenic alpine hiking along the Five Lakes Trail in Zermatt and the Eiger Trail below the north face.
- Paragliding over the turquoise waters of Interlaken and Thun.
- Mountain biking, via ferrata climbing routes, and high-altitude skiing.

## Food & Alpine Dining
- **Swiss Cheese Fondue** — Bubbling pot of Gruyère and Emmental melted in white wine and kirsch, served with crusty bread cubes.
- **Raclette** — Melted wheel of alpine cheese scraped over steamed potatoes, cornichons, and pickled onions.
- **Rösti** — Crispy golden grated potato pancake topped with fried egg, alpine bacon, and melted mountain cheese.
- **Swiss Artisan Chocolate** — Handcrafted truffles, pralines, and hot chocolate in alpine chalets.

## Travel Tips
- Best season for hiking and green meadows: June to September. For skiing: December to April.
- Swiss Travel Pass offers unlimited travel across the national rail network, panoramic trains, mountain buses, and lake ferries.
