# Jaipur Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Amber Fort & Palace** — UNESCO World Heritage hilltop fortress constructed in pink and yellow sandstone, featuring the mirror-studded Sheesh Mahal, Diwan-e-Aam, and panoramic reflections over Maota Lake.
- **Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)** — Five-story pink honeycomb facade with 953 intricately carved jharokhas (casements) designed for royal women to observe street processions without being seen.
- **City Palace Complex** — The regal seat of the Maharaja of Jaipur, featuring Mubarak Mahal, Chandra Mahal, royal courtyards, and museum galleries of royal textiles and armor.
- **Nahargarh Fort** — Hilltop ramparts on the Aravalli range delivering breathtaking sunset vistas across the entire pink city grid.
- **Jantar Mantar** — 18th-century UNESCO World Heritage astronomical observatory housing the world's largest stone sundial (Vrihat Samrat Yantra).
- **Jal Mahal** — Serene water palace appearing to float in the center of Man Sagar Lake, framed by the Aravalli hills.
- **Albert Hall Museum** — The oldest museum in Rajasthan, showcasing Indo-Saracenic architecture, Persian carpets, royal antiquities, and Egyptian relics.
- **Patrika Gate** — Opulently painted circular archway displaying colorful murals of Rajasthan's heritage, palaces, and royal history.

## Culture, Heritage & Arts
- Heritage walking tours through the walled old city, discovering centuries-old havelis, spice merchants, and brass metalworkers.
- Traditional block printing workshops in Sanganer and Bagru; blue pottery artisan studios.
- Shopping in Johari Bazaar for precious gemstones, silver jewelry, and Bandhani silk sarees.

## Food & Royal Dining
- **Dal Baati Churma** — Signature Rajasthani baked wheat dumplings served with spiced five-lentil curry, garlic chutney, and sweetened crushed churma.
- **Pyaaz Kachori & Mirchi Vada** — Crisp flaky pastries stuffed with spiced caramelized onions, best sampled at Rawat Misthan Bhandar.
- **Ghevar & Mawa Kachori** — Traditional disc-shaped honeycomb sweet soaked in saffron syrup, iconic at Laxmi Misthan Bhandar (LMB) in Johari Bazaar.
- **Chokhi Dhani** — Immersive ethnic Rajasthani cultural village with traditional folk dance (Ghoomar, Kalbelia), puppet shows, and royal dining on leaf platters.
- **Lassiwala (MI Road)** — Rich, thick creamy curd lassi served in traditional clay kulhads since 1944.

## Travel Tips
- Best season: October to March (mild, pleasant daytime temperatures).
- Moderate pace itineraries allow unhurried visits to 2 to 3 major heritage sites each day with midday rests.
- Composite monument passes cover Amber Fort, Albert Hall, Jantar Mantar, Nahargarh, and Hawa Mahal.
