# Manali Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Solang Valley** — Premier high-altitude adventure hub located 14 km from Manali; famous for paragliding, zorbing, quad biking, and winter skiing with panoramic snow-capped mountain views.
- **Rohtang Pass** — Legendary mountain pass at 3,978m elevation offering dramatic Himalayan glacial landscapes, high-altitude snow treks, and gateway vistas to Lahaul and Spiti valleys.
- **Jogini Waterfall** — Scenic pine-forested nature trek starting from Vashisht village leading to a cascading multi-tiered waterfall with serene valley views.
- **Hampta Pass Base & Trekking Route** — Breathtaking crossover trek from lush Kullu Valley forests into the stark, rugged peaks of Chandra Valley.
- **Hadimba Devi Temple** — 16th-century four-tiered wooden pagoda temple set inside the tranquil Dhungri cedar (deodar) sanctuary.
- **Manu Temple & Old Manali Village** — Historic stone-and-timber village overlooking the Manalsu river, featuring bohemian artisan bakeries, acoustic live music, and scenic cafes.
- **Naggar Castle** — Ancient wooden and stone fortress overlooking the Beas valley, celebrated for its Nicholas Roerich art gallery and traditional Himachali architecture.
- **Beas River Rapids** — Exciting Grade II and III white water rafting stretches between Paki and Jhiri.

## Nature & Outdoor Adventure
- High-altitude trekking along Hampta Pass, Bhrigu Lake, and Beas Kund.
- Paragliding over Solang Valley and Dobhi slopes with licensed tandem pilots.
- Forest bathing and peaceful morning walks in Van Vihar national deodar reserve.
- Natural thermal sulfur springs in Vashisht for deep muscular relaxation after mountain hikes.

## Food & Local Cuisine
- **Himachali Siddu** — Steamed fermented wheat bread stuffed with spiced crushed walnuts, poppy seeds, or paneer, served with desi ghee and mint chutney.
- **Kullu Trout Fish** — Fresh river trout pan-fried in butter with local lemon and mountain herbs.
- **Himachali Dham** — Traditional vegetarian celebratory feast of Madra (chickpeas in spiced yogurt), Mah ki Dal, and sweet rice.
- **Old Manali Cafes** — Wood-fired pizza, freshly baked apple crumble, Himalayan herbal teas, and specialty pour-overs.

## Travel Tips
- Best season for trekking & outdoors: May to October. For snow activities: December to February.
- Fast-paced adventure itineraries should factor in proper hydration and altitude acclimatization.
- Rent local 4x4 vehicles for Rohtang Pass permits and high-altitude excursions.
