# Bali Travel Guide

## Top attractions
- Tegalalang Rice Terrace — iconic stepped rice fields near Ubud.
- Uluwatu Temple — cliff-top sea temple, best at sunset.
- Tanah Lot — offshore rock temple on the west coast.
- Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary, Ubud.

## Beaches
- Seminyak and Canggu for surf, beach clubs and nightlife.
- Nusa Dua for calm water and family-friendly resorts.
- Uluwatu's hidden coves for dramatic cliffs.

## Food
- Babi guling (roast suckling pig), nasi campur, and fresh seafood in Jimbaran.
- Warungs (local eateries) are cheap and authentic.

## Tips
- Best season: April–October (dry season).
- Rent a scooter for flexibility; use Gojek/Grab for rides.
- Dress respectfully at temples (sarong required).
