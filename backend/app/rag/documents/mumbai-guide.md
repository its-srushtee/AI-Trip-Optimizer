# Mumbai Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Gateway of India & Colaba Waterfront** — Monumental 26m basalt archway on the Arabian Sea harbor, flanked by the historic Taj Mahal Palace hotel.
- **Marine Drive & Chowpatty Beach** — Celebrated 3.6km C-shaped coastal promenade known as the Queen's Necklace, overlooking sunset sea breezes and sandy shores.
- **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya (CSMVS)** — Grand Indo-Saracenic museum housing extensive collections of ancient Indian art, sculptures, and miniature paintings.
- **Elephanta Caves** — UNESCO World Heritage rock-cut cave temples on Elephanta Island dedicated to Lord Shiva, featuring the colossal Trimurti sculpture.
- **Bandra Bandstand & Carter Road** — Vibrant seaside promenades with sweeping ocean vistas, trendy cafes, and heritage Portuguese village alleys.
- **Lower Parel & Bandra Nightlife Hub** — Energetic nightlife district with craft cocktail bars, music lounges, and rooftop evening venues.
- **Colaba Causeway & Crawford Market** — Legendary shopping bazaar famous for street fashion, vintage brass handicrafts, antiques, and artisan jewelry.
- **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT)** — UNESCO World Heritage Victorian Gothic revival railway headquarters with monumental stone domes.

## Food & Nightlife
- **Mumbai Street Food** — Pao Bhaji, Vada Pav, Pani Puri, and Bhel Puri along bustling beach stalls.
- **Colaba & Bandra Dining** — Seafood specialties, craft cocktail bars, and energetic evening lounges.
