# Kerala Travel Guide

## Overview & Top Attractions
- **Alleppey Backwaters & Vembanad Lake** — Serene palm-fringed labyrinth of emerald backwater canals, traditional kettuvallam houseboats, and tranquil lagoons.
- **Kovalam & Marari Beaches** — Crescent-shaped sandy coastline lined with coconut palms, gentle Arabian sea waves, and Ayurvedic wellness pavilions.
- **Munnar Tea Plantations & Eravikulam** — Misty rolling mountain slopes, sprawling high-altitude tea estates, and habitat of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr.
- **Fort Kochi Heritage Quarter** — Historic coastal trading port featuring Chinese fishing nets, colonial Portuguese bungalows, and Mattancherry Palace.
- **Athirappilly Waterfalls** — Dramatic 80-foot wide curtain waterfall cascading through lush Sholayar rainforest reserves.
- **Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary (Thekkady)** — Protected national park surrounding Lake Periyar, home to wild elephant herds and aromatic spice plantations.

## Nature, Wellness & Coastal Leisure
- Traditional Ayurvedic rejuvenation massages, Panchakarma wellness therapies, and herbal steam baths.
- Peaceful canoe and kayak paddles through narrow village waterways shaded by dense mangrove groves.
- Unhurried sunset walks along pristine golden beach stretches.

## Food & Coastal Dining
- **Kerala Sadya** — Traditional feast served on a fresh banana leaf featuring red matta rice, avial, sambar, thoran, and payasam.
- **Malabar Parotta & Beef / Fish Curry** — Flaky layered flatbread served with fiery coconut-coriander fish curry or vegetable stew.
- **Appam & Vegetable Ishtu** — Fermented rice lace hoppers paired with aromatic coconut milk vegetable stew.
