# Paris Travel Guide

## Top attractions
- Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Musée d'Orsay.
- Notre-Dame Cathedral and Sainte-Chapelle.
- Montmartre and the Sacré-Cœur basilica.

## Food
- Bistros and brasseries, croissants, macarons.
- Marché d'Aligre for a classic market experience.

## Tips
- Best season: April–June and September–October.
- Get the Paris Museum Pass for major museums.
- The Métro is the fastest way around the city.
